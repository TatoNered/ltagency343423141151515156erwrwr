## First meeting

We come to you, in Belgrade and the surrounding area. It takes about an hour and we sell nothing in it — we ask what you do, where customers come from now, and what people ask when they call. Remote works too.

## Before you write

If you have a site, send the URL and get a [free review](/en/free-review/) within two working days. That way the first conversation starts from specifics.

If there is no site yet, write anyway — we will first check whether there is enough search demand in your sector to justify the investment.

## What to include in your first message

So the first reply is substance rather than "let's schedule a call":

- your website address, if you have one
- one sentence on which service earns you the most
- where customers come from now — referral, ads, marketplace
- whether a Google Business Profile exists and who controls it

That is enough for us to come back within two working days with something concrete.

## Working language

Correspondence, reports and invoices in English. Website copy is written in Serbian and proofread by a native speaker — that part is not negotiable, because machine-translated pages rank poorly and read badly to local customers.

Most of our clients are Serbian trades and service businesses: metalworkers, painters, carpenters, plumbers, electricians, driving schools, estate agencies, furniture showrooms. Alongside them we work with foreign-owned companies operating in Serbia.

## How we work

**Prices are public.** The [price list](/en/pricing/) is on the site with an explanation of what drives the number. A supplier who will not quote until they have seen your company is telling you something about how that quote gets made.

**We say when the work is not worth buying.** The pricing page carries a list of situations where monthly SEO makes no sense. We would rather lose the job at the start than explain the absence of results four months in.

**We measure enquiries, not positions.** The monthly report opens with calls and messages received.

**No lock-in.** Thirty days' notice, and you leave with everything that was built.

## Languages

Serbian, Russian and English. Serbian copy is written and proofread by a native speaker — not a formality: text written by a foreigner is obvious to a local reader within the first paragraph, and to Google as well.

## What we do not do

Brand identity, video, social media management. We do not sell link packages or guaranteed rankings.

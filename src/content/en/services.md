## Websites

A company site in Serbian, structured for search from day one. One page per service that people search for separately — a single page cannot rank for three different queries.

Copy is written by a person and proofread by a native speaker. Machine translation is obvious to local readers, and to Google.

**From €400, three weeks.** Online shops from €900, four to six weeks.

## Monthly SEO

Technical work, new pages, local visibility, links. The monthly report leads with enquiries received, not with ranking tables.

**From €300 per month.** First measurable change in three to four months.

## Google Business Profile

For service businesses the map block sits above the organic results, and a large share of people call straight from the card without opening any website.

**€150 one-off**, one week of work, visible change within two to three weeks.

## What we do not do

Logos and brand identity, video production, social media management. We do not sell link packages or guaranteed positions.

## Working across languages

If you need several languages, we recommend this split: the Serbian version complete, English and Russian kept short, five to seven pages each, written for their own audience rather than translated. A full mirror in three languages costs roughly three times as much and rarely pays for itself.

## What you get

- **Technical issues.** What stops Google reading and rendering the site properly: speed, mobile, indexing, duplication.
- **Content gaps.** Services people search for that have no page on your site.
- **Competitors.** Three companies ranking above you in Serbian search, and what they do differently.
- **Priority order.** What would move first, what second, and what can wait a year.

Two to three pages. We do not send eighty-page tool exports; nobody reads them and they say nothing.

## How it works

1. You send the URL and one sentence about which service matters most to you commercially.
2. We review the site, the search landscape and the competition. About ninety minutes of work.
3. Within two working days the document arrives by email.
4. If you have questions, we answer. If you do not reply, we do not call you.

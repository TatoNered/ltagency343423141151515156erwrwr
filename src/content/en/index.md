## Why foreign companies come to us

The usual situation: the website exists in English, the Serbian version is machine translated, and the Google business profile was never claimed. Local customers search in Serbian and find a competitor instead.

What we do about it: a Serbian version written and proofread by a native speaker, a properly configured business profile, and monthly work on visibility. Reporting and communication stay in English.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do we really need a Serbian version?
For most sectors, yes. Serbian-language queries carry the bulk of commercial search volume in this market, and machine-translated pages rank poorly and read badly to local customers. If your audience is strictly expatriate or B2B international, we will tell you that a Serbian version is lower priority.

### Can you invoice a foreign company?
Yes. We invoice from a Serbian company; details are on the [contact page](/en/contact/).

### What languages do you work in?
Serbian, Russian and English. We do not take on other languages, because we cannot verify the quality of the text, and bad copy in a language you cannot check does more harm than no copy at all.

## What drives the price

- **Site size.** Up to 20 pages is the base price. Above a hundred, the work changes shape: category templates rather than page-by-page editing.
- **Whether a developer is needed.** Standard admin access, no developer. Code-level changes, €100–150 per month extra or a task for your own team.
- **New pages per month.** Two in the local package, four in growth. Copy is written by a person and is the largest part of the monthly work.
- **Sector competition.** Different queries take different amounts of work for the same movement.

## When SEO is not worth buying

- **No search demand.** Some sectors simply are not searched for. The site matters for credibility; monthly SEO will not move anything.
- **Existing enquiries unanswered.** If five calls a week already go unreturned, visibility is not your constraint.
- **Margin too thin.** At €40 profit per job you need over a hundred new jobs monthly just to cover €300.
- **No six-month horizon.** The first three to four months are investment without return. If you are looking at next month, paid ads are the honest answer.
